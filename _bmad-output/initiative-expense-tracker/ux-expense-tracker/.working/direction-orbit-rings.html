Orbit Rings

Orbit Rings makes budgets feel like fitness goals. Each category is a progress ring you want to keep from closing, drawn on frosted glass over a soft aurora glow. The shape language is purely circular: rings, orbs, a floating pill nav and a round glowing FAB. Medium-weight type keeps it calm and premium. Motion feels like orbit: rings sweep in, an exceeded budget draws a second red "lap", and the aurora drifts slowly behind the content.

Compose / M3 notes
  • Rings: Canvas drawArc with StrokeCap.Round and a 10–12% stroke. The exceeded "lap" is a second inner arc. Use a Brush.sweepGradient for the hero.
  • Glass: a translucent Surface with a 1.dp light border. Use Modifier.blur or RenderEffect on API 31+, and fall back to an opaque tonal surface on minSdk 24.
  • Nav: a floating pill (RoundedCornerShape(50)) is a custom NavigationBar. The selected item expands to show its label, so semantics stay intact for TalkBack.
  • Motion: animateFloatAsState with a 900 ms sweep for rings and a staggered tile entrance. The aurora drifts slowly with infiniteTransition, which is disabled when "Remove animations" is on.
  • Large text: the 2-column grid collapses to 1 column (BoxWithConstraints or adaptive by width), and the rings shrink to 4.dp-relative sizes.
