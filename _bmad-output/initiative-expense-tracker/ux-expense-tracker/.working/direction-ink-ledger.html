Ink Ledger

Ink Ledger borrows the restraint of a private-bank passbook (serif display numerals, hairline rules, paper and ink) so the app feels as discreet as its privacy promise. The fun is in the details: a rubber "Exceeded" stamp, a † footnote pointing to the Inbox, and a byline that greets Shylock by name. It is the densest direction: five budgets fit above the fold. Motion is slow and inky, with lines that draw left to right and stamps that thunk in.

Compose / M3 notes
  • Type: the serif display uses FontFamily.Serif (Noto Serif on Android) or a bundled Fraunces/Newsreader. Labels use the sans at 600 in uppercase with +0.14em tracking.
  • Shapes: almost square (4.dp). Rows have no cards, only 1.dp HorizontalDividers. The FAB is an ExtendedFloatingActionButton with RoundedCornerShape(6.dp).
  • Motion: tween(600, FastOutSlowIn) line draw. The stamp enters with scale 1.4 to 1, rotates to −8°, and fires a light haptic.
  • States: WARNING shows an amber ◆ 80%+ marker and a dotted underline. EXCEEDED shows a rotated oxblood stamp, a negative amount and a line that runs past the edge. Sync is a gold byline under the masthead.
  • Large text: amounts drop below names, display numerals use a capped scale, and the nav shows only the selected label.
