Sticker Book

Sticker Book is the loudest direction: bold outlines, hard offset shadows and flat, confident colour, like a well-designed indie zine. The playfulness comes from slapped-on stickers ("85%!", "Over by ₹450!", a ₹5,000-left starburst). The premium comes from strict grid discipline, black-on-colour contrast and chunky segmented meters that read at a glance. Tiles are compact and the overspent budget gets a full-width red banner. Motion is tactile: shadows collapse when you press, and stickers wiggle once on entry.

Compose / M3 notes
  • Shapes: RoundedCornerShape(14.dp) with a 2.5.dp border. The hard shadow is a duplicate shape drawn offset by 4.dp in drawBehind (no blur), and it collapses to 0 on press via interactionSource.
  • Type: the system sans at Black (900) in uppercase for headings and amounts, with −0.02em tracking. Text on colour tiles is always #111111 to hold 7:1+ contrast in both themes.
  • Meters: 10 segments (a Row of weighted Boxes) so progress also reads without colour. EXCEEDED fills all 10 on red stripes.
  • Motion: snap tween(90 ms) press-down. Stickers rotate ±4° once with a spring on first composition, and stay static when reduce-motion is on.
  • Large text: the 2-column grid becomes 1 column, the starburst moves into flow, and the nav keeps only the selected label.
