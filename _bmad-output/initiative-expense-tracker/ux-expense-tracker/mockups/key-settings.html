Settings

The utility tab. Shylock can change his Currency and the name in his greeting, see which Google account and Expense Sheet the app uses, open the Sheet, and sign out. The account tile is also where a sync error or a needed sign-in is repeated.

← All key screens

State 1 · Settings

Settings selected in the pill nav (Inbox badge still live). Currency is ₹ INR, the display name is Shylock. The Google account tile shows the signed-in email and the Expense Sheet in use, with “Open my Sheet”. Sign out sits last. No FAB on this tab.

State 2 · Sign out with a change waiting to sync

Tapping Sign out while 1 Expense is Pending Sync opens this warning first. Cancel keeps everything. Sign out cancels sync, erases all local data and returns to Sign-in. With nothing pending, Sign out goes straight to Sign-in.

State 3a · Sync error on the account

The account tile repeats the sync pill message. A tap starts a sync. Local data stays and is never discarded.

State 3b · Needs consent on the account

Google access needs Shylock’s consent again. Background sync has stopped. A tap reopens the Google consent prompt. Until it is granted, the same line shows in the Home and Expenses sync pill.

Compose / M3 notes
  • Layout: a LazyColumn of glass surfaces under the “Settings” title (M3 Title Large), with bottom padding so Sign out clears the floating nav.
  • Currency: ExposedDropdownMenuBox with an OutlinedTextField (read-only). It lists only currencies with two decimal places. Changing it changes the symbol and formatting only.
  • Display name: OutlinedTextField, saved on IME Done or focus loss. Stored through the repository, not read from prefs in the UI.
  • Open my Sheet: an Intent.ACTION_VIEW on the Sheet URL, which opens Google Sheets if installed, otherwise the browser.
  • Sign out: if pendingSyncCount > 0, show an AlertDialog first (error-container icon orb, Cancel as TextButton in primary, Sign out filled in error). Otherwise sign out at once.
  • Sync error and needs consent: the account tile shows the same message and action as the header sync pill, with an icon plus text so it never relies on color.
