State 1 · This month (October 2026)

Period filter on “This month”. The chart covers all Categories. Rows show spent, Amount Left and budget, with WARNING and EXCEEDED states. The inbox hint and the sync pill sit above the list. The add-expense FAB is shown.

State 2 · Custom range (5 Oct – 20 Oct)

Period filter on “Custom range”. The chart and the rows show spending only for the range. There is no budget, no Amount Left, no inner budget orbit and no warning or exceeded styling. Each row ring shows that Category's share of the range's spending.

Compose / M3 notes
  • Chart: one Canvas with drawArc per Category segment (StrokeCap.Butt, 2° gaps) for the outer ring, and a second, thinner arc with Brush.sweepGradient(h1→h2) for budget used. The inner arc is omitted for custom ranges.
  • Period filter: SingleChoiceSegmentedButtonRow. “Previous months” opens a DropdownMenu (Sep 2026, Aug 2026 …) and “Custom range” opens a DateRangePicker. The selected segment shows the chosen range.
  • Category row: Surface(onClick) with RoundedCornerShape(50%) glass, so the whole row is one touch target with the merged semantics label shown in aria-label. Over budget: the inner red arc draws the overspend fraction (11%).
  • Large text: the chart stacks above its legend, the row amount wraps under the name, the sync pill wraps under the greeting, and the nav shows only the selected label.
