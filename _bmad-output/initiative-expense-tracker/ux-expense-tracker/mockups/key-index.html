Key screens

Six key screens for Shylock in October 2026, built from the owner's chosen mix: Orbit Rings structure (frosted glass tiles over a soft aurora, progress rings with a red second lap when over budget, a floating pill nav, circular shapes, medium-weight type) and Jelly Pop colours (grape-to-bubblegum hero gradient, cream and grape-black canvases, candy Category colours). Every file shows light and dark side by side. Home also has a 320 dp frame at 1.75× font scale.

Home

This month and Custom range (5 Oct – 20 Oct). Segmented ring chart, Category rows with Amount Left, inbox hint, sync pill. Light, dark and 320 dp at 1.75×.

Brand over the aurora, one “Continue with Google” button and the privacy line, then “Setting up your Expense Sheet…”. Light, dark and 320 dp at 1.75×.

Open key-signin.html →Settings

Currency, display name, Google account and Expense Sheet with Open my Sheet, Sign out. The sign-out warning when a change is waiting to sync, plus sync error and needs consent on the account.

Open key-settings.html →
Shared rules shown across the tab screens
  • Floating pill nav with 5 tabs: Home · Expenses · Inbox (badge) · Budgets · Settings. The selected tab expands to show its label.
  • The round add-expense FAB appears on Home and Expenses only.
  • WARNING at 80% of a Category budget (amber glow) and EXCEEDED at 100% (red glow plus a red second lap on the ring).
  • Unsynced rows are marked “Waiting to sync”, and the header pill counts them.
