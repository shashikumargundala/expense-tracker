Jelly Pop

Jelly Pop treats money like a toy you are proud of: heavy rounded type, squircle cards with a soft "jelly lip", and a grape-to-bubblegum hero that feels bouncy without looking cheap. Every category gets its own candy tint, so you can read the screen by colour before you read a number. Cards that need attention float to the top. Motion is springy: bars fill like liquid and the FAB squashes when pressed.

Compose / M3 notes
  • Shapes: RoundedCornerShape(24.dp) cards plus a 2.dp bottom "lip" drawn with Modifier.drawBehind. FAB uses a 20.dp squircle.
  • Type: the system sans at ExtraBold/Black (800–900) with −0.02em tracking. Amounts use tabular figures (fontFeatureSettings = "tnum").
  • Motion: spring(dampingRatio = 0.5) on bar fill and FAB press-scale (0.92). Cards stagger in, 40 ms apart.
  • States: WARNING gets an amber chip ("85% used · heads up"). EXCEEDED gets a rose card, a solid red chip and a striped full bar. Sync shows as an amber pill in the header.
  • Large text: the amount wraps below the name, and the nav shows only the selected label (M3 NavigationBar alwaysShowLabel = false).
