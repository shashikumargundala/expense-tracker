# Expense Tracker privacy policy

*Last updated: 28 October 2026*

Expense Tracker is a personal expense tracker for Android. It's built so that your financial data belongs to you: it lives on your phone and in your own Google Drive, and nowhere else.

## What the app uses

- **Your Google account email and account ID.** When you sign in with Google, the app keeps your account's email and ID on your phone so it knows who is signed in. They aren't sent anywhere except to Google, as part of signing in.
- **The expenses, categories and budgets you enter** *(arriving in upcoming versions; the current test build only signs you in)*. They're stored on your phone and synced to a spreadsheet, your "Expense Sheet", that the app creates in **your own Google Drive**.

## Where your data goes

- **On your phone,** in the app's private storage. Android backup of the app's data is turned off.
- **In your Google Drive** *(once expense syncing arrives)*, in a spreadsheet the app creates. The app uses Google's `drive.file` permission, so it can only see files it created itself. It can't see your other Drive files.
- **Nowhere else.** The app has no servers of its own. The developer never receives your data.

## What the app doesn't do

- No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking libraries.
- No ads.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

## Deleting your data

- **On your phone:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings.
- **In Google Drive:** once the app has created your Expense Sheet, delete that spreadsheet like any other file.
- **App access to your Google account:** remove it at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Changes

If a future version adds features that use more data (for example reading bank SMS to suggest expenses), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date will change.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
